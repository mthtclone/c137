A short walking simulator about exploring a quiet place and taking it in. Playtime is about 20–30 minutes.

About

This is a small atmospheric environment we attempted to create as part of the major project for our game development class.

It is admittedly quite amateur, but I hope you’ll appreciate our attempt to build something using a sophisticated game engine like Unity and the experience we gained through the process.
